Who We Are

A School That Inspires a Brighter Tomorrow

Our Lady of Mercy High School is presented here as a learning community committed to academic growth, personal responsibility, and the development of each learner's potential.

We believe that education should develop the mind, nurture good character, and prepare students to contribute positively to society.

  • Quality teaching and learning
  • Respect, discipline, and responsibility
  • Creativity, leadership, and teamwork
